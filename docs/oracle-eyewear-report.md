# Cutler and Gross stock and sales report

In authenticated Oracle chat, enter **Cutler and Gross stock and sales
report**. It runs in the background and returns an owner-scoped workbook
for the last three London calendar years through yesterday.

The stock reader paginates the exact Shopify **Sunglasses** collection and
all Shopify products, variants and active locations. Collection membership
or a Cutler and Gross title qualifies, subject to the accessory and repair
exclusions. The stock read is time stamped; available units are not proof
of Online Store sellability. A failed collection lookup leaves title-based
coverage available and is disclosed in Coverage.

Sales use source-qualified orders and deduplicated lines. Historical branded
titles and approved alias/family links can qualify without a live listing.
Conflicting mappings are held separate. Approved parent families group
styles, but do not establish colour identity across sources. Source variant
references and observed colour labels are retained separately when no
verified colour equivalence exists. Square uses the existing conservative
payment-based channel rules; unsupported or conflicting evidence stays Unknown.

Totals count each source order once; style counts and currency counts must
not be added to reproduce the overall count. Line sales do not include the
rest of the basket, use native currencies and tax treatment, and are not
refund-adjusted. Shopify returned pairs are observed quantities linked to
the exact sale line where a successful refund is retained. A missing refund
is not proof of zero returns; net pairs and historical sell-through remain
unavailable without compatible verified evidence.

Coverage shows each requested month and source, eligible orders represented
by warehouse data, missing order lines, and unresolved mappings. Coverage
is observational and never certifies a missing month as zero. Each source
has a read timeout, a bytes limit and a result bound. A source that fails or
exceeds its bound is disclosed without replacing other sources or delivering
truncated totals. No collection, inventory updates or supplier orders run.
